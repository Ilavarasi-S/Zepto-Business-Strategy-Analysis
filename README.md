Zepto – Business Strategy Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project analyses Zepto's business strategy and market position in India's quick-commerce industry using qualitative and quantitative analysis.

The project focuses on understanding Zepto's business model, customer perception, competitive position, financial performance, business challenges and growth opportunities.

---

🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to analyse:

- Zepto's business model and Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
- Market gaps and customer needs
- Customer sentiment and service-related issues
- Competitive position in the quick-commerce industry
- Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats
- Financial performance and profitability challenges
- Strategic recommendations for sustainable growth

---

🔍 Analysis Performed

1. Company & Business Model Analysis
Analysed Zepto's business model, target customers and value proposition in the quick-commerce market.

2. USP & Market Gap Analysis
Identified the key factors that differentiate Zepto and the customer needs addressed by its business model.

3. Customer Sentiment Analysis
Analysed customer reviews and feedback to understand common positive experiences, complaints and service-related issues.

4. Competitor Analysis
Compared Zepto with major competitors to understand its competitive position and market challenges.

5. SWOT Analysis
Evaluated Zepto's:

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

6. Financial Performance Analysis
Analysed the relationship between business growth, revenue and profitability to understand the challenges associated with rapid expansion.

---

💡 Key Business Insight

A key insight from this analysis was the balance between:

**Growth vs Profitability**

While Zepto has experienced strong growth in the quick-commerce market, rapid expansion also creates challenges related to operational costs, competition, service reliability and profitability.

---

📊 Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis, recommendations focused on:

- Improving customer retention
- Enhancing service reliability
- Addressing customer complaints
- Improving operational efficiency
- Strengthening profit margins
- Exploring additional monetisation opportunities
- Building sustainable long-term growth
